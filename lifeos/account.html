Your account

Checking your session…

Account settings are separate from tracker preferences. The dashboard opens a separate local workspace for each signed-in account; cloud syncing is optional there. Device-only history is never uploaded automatically. Logout retains pending local changes for the same account; sign back in to resume.

Delete cloud account

This permanently deletes your cloud account and cloud records. Your account workspace may remain in this browser, but it cannot be reopened through a deleted account. Open the dashboard and download a local backup first.
