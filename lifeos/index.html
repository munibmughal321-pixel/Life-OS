A few things you might be wondering.

Where should I start?

Choose Get started to create an account, or explore the workspace first. The welcome setup helps you choose interests, a priority and a daily time intention.

Is LifeOS only for students?

No. Learning includes books, workshops and independent study. Skills can be technical, creative, practical or personal. Academic tools are optional.

Can I use it without creating an account?

You can explore the local workspace without signing in. Browser data belongs to that device and browser profile. Use the app’s backup tools to keep an external copy.

Will reminders work when I close the browser?

Web reminders depend on browser permissions and the app being available. They are not a dependable alarm when the browser is closed or the device is asleep.

Can I change my goals and interests later?

Yes. Edit your preferences, goals and learning paths as your priorities change. You don’t have to organize everything on day one.
